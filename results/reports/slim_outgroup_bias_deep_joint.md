# SLiM outgroup-bias validation (Ancestree FixedTreeInference)

Full 4-population SLiM forward sim (ingroup p0 + outgroups p1/p2/p3 with nested splits) at rescaled $N_e = 1000$, joined back to the human-ape $N_e \approx 10^4$ scale by $s = N_\text{target}/N_\text{slim} = 10$. Split times at the target scale: $T_1, T_2, T_3 = (3\cdot 10^5, 6\cdot 10^5, 9\cdot 10^5)$ gens.

**Simulator settings**

- `seq_len_total` = 100000
- `mu_target` = 1.25e-08
- `rec_rate_target` = 1e-08
- `Ne_target` = 10000
- `N_e_slim` = 100
- `N_e_outgroup_slim` = 5
- `outgroup_split_times_target` = [4000000.0, 8000000.0, 12000000.0]
- `n_ingroup_haps` = 20
- `seed` = 42
- `n_chunks` = 10
- `seq_len_per_chunk` = 10000
- `time_scaling` = 100.0
- `mu_slim` = 1.2499999999999999e-06
- `rho_slim` = 1e-06
- `outgroup_split_times_slim` = [40000, 80000, 120000]
